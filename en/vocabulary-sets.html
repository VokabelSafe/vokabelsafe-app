🧾 Full Freedom: Create Your Own Vocabulary Sets for Free

The biggest difference between VokabelSafe and conventional apps: While other providers charge additional fees for each new book or each language level, VokabelSafe offers an open interface.

💰 No Costs for Vocabulary Content

The decisive advantage: You don't buy vocabulary packages from us. Thanks to VokabelSafe's open interface, you can create an unlimited number of sets for your entire school life, studies or career – without ever having to spend money on digital learning content again.

Whether vocabulary from a textbook, technical terms or sentences for a vacation: creating and importing your own lists is permanently free of charge. This saves real money over the years, as there are no expensive license fees for pre-made packages.

You determine exactly what is learned. Preparing on a PC or via AI saves time and ensures that you practice exactly the content you really need.

📂 Cleverly Manage and Switch Vocabulary Sets

In VokabelSafe, you can easily organize your vocabulary so that learning always remains clear. You have two options when importing your files:

  • Expand existing set: Simply import new words into your current set. They will then be automatically appended as additional entries.
  • Create a completely new set: If you want to start a new topic or school year, go to the gear (Settings) in the top right of the app → "Manage Vocabulary Sets". There you can create a new, empty set and then specifically fill it with your new vocabulary.

The best part: On the start page of the app, you can switch back and forth between your various vocabulary sets at any time by clicking on the set name. This way, you always have exactly what you want to learn right in front of you.

💡 Practical Tips for Organization:

  • For students: Start a separate set for each school year (e.g. "Spanish 7th grade"). This keeps the app perfectly structured over the years and old vocabulary doesn't interfere with current material.
  • For adults: Separate strictly by language or area of application (e.g. "Business Spanish", "French Vacation" or "Medical Technical Terms").
