✉️ Contact and Support

If you have any questions about the app, the free promotion or topics such as data protection, import and export, you can write to us at any time. We are happy to help and answer as clearly and simply as possible.

We are aware that many people hesitate when writing to an unknown address. That's why we say it very clearly: We handle your email address just as carefully as the data in our app.

Your email address will never be passed on to third parties. No sale, no passing on, no tracking.

You may also write to us if you only have a short question or would like to inform yourself without obligation.

Trust and Privacy

VokabelSafe stands for a quiet and safe learning environment. This claim also applies to support: respectful contact, clear answers and responsible handling of your data.
