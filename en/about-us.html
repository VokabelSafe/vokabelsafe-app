💬 Developed by Parents – The story behind VokabelSafe

VokabelSafe is not an anonymous software product, but a real passion project that arose directly from our daily family life.

We develop this app as private individuals for our own daughter. Our goal is to make learning vocabulary for school as easy and stress-free as possible for her.

Many existing apps were too overloaded, inflexible or full of advertising, which is particularly unsuitable for children. That's why we created a solution that concentrates on the essentials: Quiet, focused learning without distraction and without account requirements.

What began as a small help for everyday school life has since become a real family project. Our daughter uses the app daily – whether at home, in the car or on the way to school. She has long been more than just a user: she constantly provides us with new ideas and suggestions for improvement from a child's perspective and even implements some of them together with us.

In this way, VokabelSafe continues to grow – from parents for children, with a lot of heart and directly from practice.
