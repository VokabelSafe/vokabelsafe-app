🛡️ Unique concept: Two separate worlds

VokabelSafe is a digital shelter for your child. For maximum security and efficiency, we strictly separate the child's learning environment and content creation:

👶 Kids App: 100% Safe

The app on the student's cell phone is absolutely ad-free and offline. It requires 0% permissions, avoids tracking and does not collect any data. Pure focus on learning.

💻 Web Portal: Full Power

On our website, parents & teachers use modern 🤖 generators to create ready-made vocabulary sets in seconds from copied book texts (OCR) or topics via AI prompt – completely free of charge.

🛡️ Privacy Concept

Important note on backup: Since VokabelSafe does not use a cloud account, all vocabulary words are stored exclusively on your device. To ensure that nothing is lost if you change or lose your phone, the app offers a simple manual backup function. Back up your data regularly so that you can transfer it to a new device at any time.

🌍 Native language as a bridge – Learn vocabulary more efficiently

VokabelSafe helps children with a migration background learn foreign languages more efficiently. Use the native language as a bridge. Our website offers a special function for this – this is how it works with our 📚 Textbook Guide:

  • Photograph the textbook: Simply capture the vocabulary page with your mobile phone.
  • AI adds native language: Our generators such as the 📸 OCR Prompt Generator create the appropriate command so that the AI automatically adds the translation in the language of origin (e.g. Ukrainian, Arabic or Turkish) as a memory aid – even if it is not in the book!
  • Easy import: The finished set lands in the app in seconds.

Similarly, our ✨ Topic Generator can be used to create complete vocabulary sets on any topic, in which the native language can be automatically added as a bridge.
