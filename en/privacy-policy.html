🛡️ Maximum Privacy: 0% Data, 100% Security

VokabelSafe sets new standards in protecting your child: We radically dispense with the collection of data. For families and schools, privacy is not an extra, but the basic requirement of our entire concept.

  • No account, no registration: Your child can start immediately – without an email address, without a password and without giving a name.
  • Absolute anonymity: We do not create user profiles and do not track learning behavior outside of your device.
  • No disclosure: Where no data is collected, no data can be sold, hacked or abused.

There is no tracking, no advertising profiles and no hidden analysis services in the background. All vocabulary and progress remain exclusively in your personal "safe" – locally on your smartphone or tablet.

Good to know: Full control over your data also means that no automatic backup takes place in a cloud. We therefore recommend creating a manual backup regularly to protect your vocabulary in the event of a device change or loss.

🔐 0% Access: Full concentration on learning

Many apps request significantly more permissions than are necessary for the actual purpose. VokabelSafe radically follows the principle of data minimization: The app does not require any access to your private data or device functions.

  • No access to contacts or camera: We don't need to know who you know or what you look like.
  • No location tracking: Your location remains your private business.
  • No push notifications: We do not disturb your child with "learning reminders" or advertisements. Children should learn when it suits them – without digital pressure or distraction.

Important note on camera use: Our app is deliberately kept permission-free. The preparation of textbook vocabulary (via OCR text recognition) takes place exclusively on our website. In this way, parents or teachers can conveniently capture and prepare documents via the browser, while the child's learning environment on the smartphone remains maximally protected and technically isolated.

🤝 Transparency creates trust

Minimal permissions are listed in the Google Play Store for technical reasons. It is important to us that you know exactly what is behind them:

  • Network access & license check: This is used exclusively for the one-time check of your purchase via Google.
Real offline concept: VokabelSafe does not force a permanent internet connection. The app remembers your license status locally. This means: once installed, your child can continue learning completely autarkically even in flight mode, in a dead zone or on vacation.

There is no cloud requirement and no data synchronization in the background.

🚫 100% Ad-Free: Focus on the essentials

VokabelSafe is a deliberate decision against sensory overload. In our app, there are no flashing banners, no annoying commercial breaks and no lure offers.

  • No distraction: Your child can concentrate fully on the vocabulary without being torn out of the learning flow by colorful video ads.
  • Maximum security: Since no external advertising is loaded, there is no risk of your child accidentally clicking on unsuitable content or cost traps.
  • Quiet learning environment: We dispense with manipulative design elements that want to keep children at the screen longer than necessary.

Learning should be fun, but in a protected and quiet framework. With VokabelSafe, the sentence "Mom, an ad just came up" is a thing of the past.

✈️ Real Offline Learning: Anywhere and anytime

VokabelSafe does not require a permanent internet connection. Once installed, the app is your reliable companion – no matter where you are.

  • No data consumption: This saves mobile data volume and also works in dead zones.
  • Ideal for on the go: Whether in the car, on the plane or on vacation – learning vocabulary always works, even without WiFi.
  • Independence: No dependence on servers or cloud services. The app starts immediately and reacts lightning fast.

All learning content is stored locally on the device. This keeps your child independent of network coverage.

🛡️ Our Promise to Parents and Schools

VokabelSafe is a digital shelter for your child. We developed the app privately for our own daughter, who uses it voluntarily every day. Maximum privacy, ad-free and without digital pressure.

No tracking, no accounts, no data collection. 100% focus on learning.
